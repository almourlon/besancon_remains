About the project

Compiled in 1715 and now held by the Bibliothèque municipale de Besançon, BMB Ms 1054 brings together dialogues and occasional pieces written at different moments in the city’s recent past. In French as well as in Franc-Comtois and Bisontin forms of speech, monuments, winegrowers, travellers and civic figures debate the events that transformed Besançon between the late sixteenth and early eighteenth centuries.

The collection preserves the memory of a city repeatedly confronted with changes of status, allegiance and government. Yet its texts do more than record political upheaval: they reveal the endurance of local institutions, language, humour and civic identity. Reading them together makes it possible to examine how the people of Besançon understood change and how they continued to imagine what made their city distinctive.

The digital edition

This website presents work towards a critical edition of the collection. It brings manuscript images and transcriptions together, identifies textual variants where other witnesses survive, and provides the historical and material context needed to interpret the texts. The transcriptions and editorial notes remain a work in progress.
